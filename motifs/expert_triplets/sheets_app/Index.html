Instructions
  • Click the neighbourhood that is least like the other two, or press 1 2 3.
  • There's no right answer key. Go with your first impression. If all three look alike, still pick the one that differs most.
  • If an image can't be judged (blurry, bad segmentation, centre cell at the image edge, or mostly empty), click Flag unusable under it, or press Q W E. Flag as many of the three as apply, then Submit as unusable (X). Click a flag again to clear it.
  • Misclicked? Undo last (Z) brings the previous set back.
  • Stop whenever you like. Every answer is saved as you go.
